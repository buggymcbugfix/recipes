Recipe
Pulneni Chushki

Dice 4 medium or two large onions and two carrots and in the big Le Creuset casserole fry on gentle heat until golden (20-30 mins). Add two bay leaves and a tsp of herbes de Provence. Add 3 finely chopped cloves of garlic at the end.

Mix half a cup of rice with half a cup (or a bit less?) of water and microwave for 7 minutes.

Mix half of the golden onion/carrot melee with 500 g ground beef, the rice, an egg, 1 tsp paprika (preferably freshly ground), 7g salt, pepper.

Preheat oven to 180°C.

Bring the remaining melee back up to heat, add two to three tbsp of tomato concentrate. Deglaze with 1 dl of red wine, let it cook until the wine almost completely boils off. Add a normal size can of tomato and an equal amount of beef bone broth.

Lovingly stuff 4-6 peppers, which have had their tops removed and butts incised, with... well, you guessed it, the stuffing. Make meatballs from any remaining stuffing.

Puree the tomato sauce, then immerse the protagonists in it and put the lid on. Bake for 90 mins. 

Make rice.

Sprinkle peppers with parsley and serve with rice and yoghurt.
