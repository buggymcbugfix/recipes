Tiramisu'
=========

source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V9uApXYaWs4&t=513s

* 3 egg yolks
* 100 g sugar (original: 125 g)
* 250 g mascarpone
* 100 ml caffe'
* 1 pkg lady finger biscuits
* cocoa powder

tuorli + zucchero: mix 5 mins on high, add mascarpone, mix on high until the cream is fluffy. do not overmix to avoid splitting.

spread a thin layer of cream on the bottom of a dish, then layer coffee-soaked biscuits and cream. dust with cocoa powder shortly before serving.
