Lehsuni Palak Dal Tadka | Dal Palak | Lentils with Spinach
==========================================================

Source: Sonia Barton, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WlVJhysGUeY

1 cup dal, soak for 20 mins then drain and add to pressure cooker with
2.5 cup water (on top of the soaking water? schatzi seems to suggest 3-4:1)
2 tsp salt (?)
0.5 tsp haldi
roughly peel 1 tomato and add peel, retain rest
add 2 roughly chopped green chilies

pressure cook for 3-4 whistles (15 min?)

in 3 Tbspn oil, fry 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1 medium size sliced onion. cook until brown, add two whole red chillies,
add chopped spinash leaves.
cook for 4-5 mins on low flame.

Add dal, cook for 1 more minute. Done.
