Murgh Makhani (Butter Chicken)
==============================

source: [Chef Din](youtube.com/watch?v=D2xp9x5tzd8)


Chicken
-------

Grind

- 1.5 tsp (= 3 g) kashmiri red chilli
- 0.5 tsp (= 1.7 g) zeera
- 0.5 tsp (= 1.6 g) dhania

to a fine powder and add to a bowl with

- 1 Tbsp ginger-garlic paste (= 15 g)
- juice of 1 lime (30 ml)
- 0.5 tsp haldi (= 1.5 g)
- 1 tsp salt (= 7 g)
- black pepper [@vil's addition]
- 2 Tbsp mustard oil

and mix well. Add 700 g chicken, cut into fairly large cubes, mix well and marinate in the fridge for 4-12 hours.

BBQ, roast or pan-fry the chicken briefly on very high heat, so it browns on the outside and stays as juicy as possible on the inside. Set aside and keep the drippings / pan for the next step.


Butter Sauce
------------

Put the pan on a medium high heat and add

- 125 ml vegetable oil (= 0.5 cup) [if there is leftover oil from pan-frying, reduce the quantity accordingly]
- 50 g butter
- 1 small (cassia) cinnamon stick (5 g)
- 1 black cardamom pod
- 1 green cardamom pod [@vil's addition]
- 1 Tbsp ginger-garlic paste (= 15 g)
- 300 g very finely sliced onion
- 2 tsp salt (= 7 g).

Bring the heat right up and stir. Cook for 10 mins.

Soak 30 g of cashews in hot water.

Add 600 g of chopped roma tomatoes. Put lid on and let it simmer very gently for 40 mins.

Pass the mixture through a fine-mesh sieve into a bowl, use some hot water to get as much flavour out of the debris as you can.

Grind

- 1.5 tsp (= 3 g) kashmiri red chilli
- 0.5 tsp (= 1.7 g) zeera
- 0.5 tsp (= 1.6 g) dhania

to a fine powder.

In the same saucepan, heat 3 Tbsp (45 ml) vegetable oil and 30 g of butter. Add the ground spices and let them heat in the oil briefly until fragrant. Do not let them burn! Add the strained sauce and bring to a boil. 

Blend the soaked cashews with about a third of the sauce and return to the sauce, rinsing with a small amount of hot water, which you add to the sauce as well.

Add 3 whole green chilis. [@vil: You can also add them chopped, for a little extra spice, as this recipe is quite mild.]

Add 1 Tbsp of broken up kasuri methi. [@vil If it doesn't break up in your hands, grind it.]

Add 0.5 cup (= 125 ml) cream and 1 tsp (= 5 ml) of honey [@vil: original recipe uses 2 - 3 tsp, but my onions and tomatoes were already quite sweet].

Add water and/or salt according to taste. Mix in 0.5 tsp of garam masala [@vil's addition, courtesy of Auntie Sonia].

Let this simmer gently for 5 mins.


Serving
-------

Cut the chicken into bite-size pieces and simmer it in the sauce for 5–10 mins. If you choose to add some chopped coriander leaves, mix them in 2–3 mins before the dish is done, so they can wilt and marry with the sauce.

Serve with saffron rice. For that, cook some good, aged basmati rice from a south-asian grocer (the kernels should be firm and homogeneously translucent), that has been pre-soaked for a few hours at least. While the rice is cooking, grind a generous pinch of saffron with 0.25 tsp salt and 0.25 tsp sugar. Dissolve in two Tbsp of warm water. When the rice is done, fluff it such that there be no clumps. Take about a fifth of the rice and pop it in a bowl, in which you let it get cozy with the saffron water and a knob of butter. Really mix it well. Heap the bulk of the rice on a serving plate, then transfer the saffron-infused rice onto the peak and let it cover the prime real estate of rice mountain.
