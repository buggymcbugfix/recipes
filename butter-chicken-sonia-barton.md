Recipe for Butter Chicken by Sonia Barton

Contributed by: @nils

Marinating
500 grams chicken
½ lemon juice
salt
1 tbsp kashmiri red chilli powder
1 ½ tsp ginger garlic paste (1:1 ginger to garlic)
2 tsp curd (dahi or thick yoghurt)

- mix well, cover with cling wrap and let marinate for ½ hour
(when using chicken with bone, you can make cuts in the pieces)

Frying
- fry the chicken pieces with a little bit of oil on low heat (so that the chicken cooks in the moisture of the curd). When one side is golden brown, flip and cover lid & cook for 7-10 min. Set aside.

Sauce
1 medium diced onion
1 tsp ginger garlic paste
handful of chopped cashews (or almonds)
2 large tomatoes, roughly chopped

- heat 1 tbsp of oil and add 1 onion. When translucent add ginger garlic paste. Add cashews and tomatoes. Add salt to taste and mix well. Cover & cook for 2-4 minutes until tomatoes soften. Mash tomatoes a bit with spatula. Blend into smooth puree and keep aside

Final step
1 tbsp butter
2 tbsp oil
1 + 1 tsp red chilly powder
¼ tsp tumeric powder
2 tsp coriander powder
1 tbsp tomato sauce/ketchup or honey
1 tsp fenugreek leaves
little full fat cooking cream
1 tsp garam masala

- heat butter and oil in a pot. Add 1 tsp red chilly powder and tumeric and immediately add the puree and mix. Add coriander powder and 1 tsp red chilly powder and mix. Then add chicken pieces. Cover and cook for 2-3 mins until oil separates. Then add 1 tbspn of tomato sauce or honey, fenugreek leaves and garam masala, cover and cook for 2-4 minutes. Serve with  little cooking cream
