Pizza
=====

source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vf0god0LtP8

Dough
-----

1 kg high-protein flour (00)
640 g water
5 g fresh yeast (or 2.5 g dried yeast, but it's not as good)
32 g salt
a bit of olive oil for the counter

Put about half of water in stand mixer. Add all the salt to the remaining water and let it dissolve.
Dissolve fresh yeast in water in stand mixer. Add about half the flour.
Knead.

Add the salt water now and add in the remaining flour.
Knead for 5-10 mins.

Let rest for 30 mins, covered, in the stand mixer bowl, without the kneading hook.

Remove onto an oiled kitchen counter and knead into a ball. Separate into 8 balls.

Let them rise (covered) for about 2 hours @ 25°C. Alternatively, in the fridge for up to 48 hours. Remove from fridge 1-2 hours before baking.


To make 8 Pizzas
----------------

640 ml passata
pecorino or other Italian hard cheese
8 mozzarella balls
Basil
Olive oil

Spread a dough ball very thinly.
Spread 80 ml (1/3 cup) of passata on that.
Spread some grated hard cheese. 
Break 1 mozzarella ball into clumps and spread.
Add a few leaves of basil and a drizzle of olive oil.
