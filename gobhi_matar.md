Gobhi Matar
===========

Source: Sonia Barton, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ybaVzuqK4DY

300 g cauliflower florets
1 cup green peas
1 medium onion + 10 g ginger + 4 garlic cloves paste
blend 2 medium tomatoes
60 ml oil

fry cauliflower 3-4 mins, then remove
add 3cm of cinnamon + 1 indian bay leaf to oil + 1 tsp cumin seeds + 1/8 tsp asafoetida
when fragrant, add onion / garlic / ginger paste
cook 2 mins
1.5 tsp kashmiri red chili powder
1/2 tsp haldi
2 tsp dhania
1 tsp salt
when oil separates, add tomato puree
after 2 mins add green peas
cook on low for 5 mins
add fried cauliflower with a green chili cut into long diagonal stripes
add some water
let cook for 5 mins
add 1 tsp garam masala
add kasoori methi
add roughly chopped coriander leaves and let simmer for 2 mins
