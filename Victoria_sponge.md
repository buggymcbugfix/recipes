⁠Victoria sponge

* 400g butter

or

* 320g ghee
* 80g milk

melt, e.g. thaw setting 10 min

* 200g (orig. 400g) caster sugar
* 1 tsp vanilla extract
* 8 medium eggs
* salt

froth. Then add molten butter while mixing.

* 400g flour
* 2 tsp baking powder
mix in a separate bowl, then add spoon-by-spoon, but swiftly, to sugary-egg-butter-froth.

Put into two tins.

190 C 25 mins

* 1 whipped cream
* 1 cream cheese
* bit of sugar or golden syrup

Spread on bottom cake.

* raspberry jam
* fresh raspberries

but on cream, then complete sandwich. Enjoy.
