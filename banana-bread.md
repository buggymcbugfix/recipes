Banana Bread
============

SOURCE:  Karen St Amand
PREHEAT:  180
MAKES:  1 cake

INGREDIENTS:

	1/2 c 	butter (125 grams)
	1/2 c 	sugar (100 g, original 200 g)
	2	eggs
	2	banana
	1 tsp 	vanilla essence
	1 pkg 	baking soda (or 1/2 tsp)
	2 c 	flour (250 g)
	1/4 tsp 	salt
		walnuts

METHOD:

Thoroughly mix all wet ingredients. Mix all dry ingredients. Slowly stir dry ingredients into wet ingredients and add walnuts when finished. Pour batter into a baking pan lined with paper. Bake for 55 min.
