Dal
===

source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8AaGXzdve5Y

2.5 cups toor dal (1 pkg) fill up to max line on instantpot pressure cooker, add 1-2 tsp haldi. Pressure cook on high for 15 min, then let pressure release naturally (~30 min).

For tarka:

Dice 1 big onion. Purée 4 cloves of garlic, 1 knob of ginger ca. 3 by 2 cm, 2 green chilli, adding enough water to make a paste.

Put oil in 28 cm pan, fry 1 scant Tbsp cumin seeds, then add onions and fry on high heat until browned. Add 2 tsp garam masala and 1 tsp red chilli powder, mix for a few seconds. Add ginger-garlic-chilli paste, stir for a minute or two. Add 300 g of tomato purée (fresh or passata), then add water + 2 Tbsp salt. Let it come to a boil. Add 1 Tbsp kasoori methi.

Pour dal into tarka. Purée partially in nutribullet.
