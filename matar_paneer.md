Matar Paneer
============

* 1 large onion or 3 small onions, cut into half moons
* 400 g paneer
* handful of cashews (50 g?), soaked in hot water for 10 mins
* 2 bay leaves
* 1 stick of cinnamon
* 3 pods of cardamom
* 1 Tbsp ginger garlic paste
* 1 tsp kashmiri red chili powder
* 2 tomatoes, blended
* 1 tsp red chili powder (mild)
* 1.5 tsp coriander powder (or blend 2 tsp fresh coriander seeds)
* 0.5 tsp turmeric powder
* 1 cup peas
* 2 tsp salt
* 2 green chilies
* 1 tsp fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi)
* 1 tsp garam masala
* 2 Tbsp cream

Shallow fry onions until golden brown to make birista. Shallow fry paneer until golden brown (do not overfry!).
Blend soaked cashews with birista to make paste. Add a little water to the blender and add to paste. Heat leftover oil in a shallow, wide pan plus add to a total of 1/4 cup (?). Add bay leaves, cinnamon, cardamom and ginger garlic paste. Stir for 30 seconds, add tomato purée. Add red chili, coriander and turmeric. Mix. Cook until oil separates (5-10 mins). Add cashew-onion paste + water to get a gravy-like consistency. Add peas and salt. Cover and cook on low for 5 mins. Add green chilies. Add fried paneer. Mix. Add fenugreek leaves. Cover and cook for 5 mins. Add chopped coriander leaves, garam masala and cream. Cover and cook on very low heat for 4-5 mins.
